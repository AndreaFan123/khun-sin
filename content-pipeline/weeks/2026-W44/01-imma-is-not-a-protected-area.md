# IMMA 是重要棲地線索，不等於保護區

## Fact card

### Proposed angle

Explain the difference between a scientific Important Marine Mammal Area and a legally managed protected area.

### Central claim

An IMMA identifies habitat that deserves attention in planning and conservation decisions; the label does not itself create legal protection, rules, enforcement, or measured outcomes.

### Confirmed facts

- The Important Marine Mammal Areas programme defines IMMAs as discrete portions of habitat important to marine-mammal species or populations.
- The programme says identified areas have the potential to be delineated and managed for conservation.
- The identification process is scientific and is intended to help decision-makers prioritise conservation consideration.
- The programme provides an IMMA spatial layer by request under a user licence for non-commercial use; commercial use follows separate terms.

### Uncertainty and limits

- This package did not assess any individual IMMA, management plan, or conservation outcome.
- IMMA status alone does not show whether a government has designated a protected area or changed fishing, shipping, tourism, or development rules.
- The source does not establish how Taiwan authorities currently use IMMA evidence.

### Taiwan connection

The connection is contextual. For Taiwan, an important-area map could inform research or spatial planning, but local legal authority, consultation, management measures, budgets, monitoring, and enforcement would still need separate decisions.

### Reader action

When a map labels an ecologically important area, ask whether it is a scientific priority, a legal designation, or an actively managed site—and what rules and monitoring actually follow.

### Claims not to publish

- Every IMMA is a marine protected area.
- An IMMA designation automatically restricts human activity.
- Mapping an area proves that species or habitat conditions improved.
- The IMMA spatial layer can be republished or sold without a licence review.

## 繁體中文草稿

在海洋保育地圖上看到「重要海洋哺乳動物棲地」（IMMA），很容易把它理解成已經受到法律保護的海域。兩者其實不同。

IMMA 是由科學程序辨識出的重要棲地，目的是讓政府、研究者與其他決策者知道哪些區域值得優先納入保育考量。它回答的是「哪裡可能重要」，而不是直接宣布禁漁、限速、限制開發或配置執法人力。

要把科學線索變成保育成果，中間還有一段治理流程：確認主管機關與法律工具、評估利害關係人與風險、選擇管理措施、配置監測資源，並檢查措施是否真的降低威脅。只在地圖上畫出範圍，不能證明環境已經改善。

對臺灣而言，IMMA 類型的資料可以是研究或海域規劃的輸入，但不能直接當成本地管理現況的證明。讀到「重要棲地」時，可以追問三件事：這是科學評估還是法律指定？目前有哪些具體規則？成效用什麼資料追蹤？

## English adaptation

An Important Marine Mammal Area can look like a protected area on a map, but the two are not equivalent.

An IMMA is identified through a scientific process. It helps governments, researchers, and other decision-makers see where marine-mammal habitat deserves priority consideration. It does not by itself impose fishing restrictions, vessel-speed rules, development controls, enforcement, or a management budget.

Turning a scientific priority into a conservation outcome requires additional governance: a responsible authority, a legal or voluntary measure, consultation, implementation, monitoring, and evidence that the threat has changed. Drawing a boundary does not prove that conditions improved.

For Taiwan, this kind of spatial evidence may inform research or marine planning, but it does not demonstrate current local protection. When an area is labelled important, ask whether the label is scientific or legal, what management follows, and how outcomes are measured.
