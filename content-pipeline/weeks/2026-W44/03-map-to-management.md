# 從優先地圖到保育成果，中間還缺什麼？

## Fact card

### Proposed angle

Compare two international priority-setting frameworks to explain the steps between identifying ecological importance and producing a conservation outcome.

### Central claim

Priority frameworks can focus attention and organise evidence, but outcomes still depend on local authority, intervention design, implementation, monitoring, and revision.

### Confirmed facts

- IMMAs identify discrete habitat important to marine mammals for consideration in conservation decisions.
- MTSG's portfolio compares marine-turtle regional units using biological risk, threats, and conservation capacity.
- Neither framework, by itself, creates Taiwan law, a local management plan, an operating budget, or enforcement.
- Taiwan's Ocean Conservation Administration publishes marine-conservation programmes and open-government information, but this package does not identify a formal adoption of either framework.

### Uncertainty and limits

- The comparison is conceptual and does not evaluate a specific Taiwan site.
- Local laws may use other datasets, categories, or consultation processes.
- “Priority” can refer to ecological importance, urgency, feasibility, or resource allocation; those meanings should not be collapsed.

### Taiwan connection

The connection is contextual. A Taiwan application would need to identify the competent authority, applicable legal mechanism, affected users, implementable measures, baseline, indicators, and public reporting cycle.

### Reader action

Read a conservation map as the start of a decision chain: evidence → priority → authority → measure → implementation → monitoring → adjustment.

### Claims not to publish

- International priority maps are binding on Taiwan.
- Scientific importance alone determines the correct regulation.
- A protected-area announcement is equivalent to a verified ecological outcome.
- The source maps can be incorporated into a paid product without licence review.

## 繁體中文草稿

保育地圖最擅長回答的是「注意力應該放在哪裡」，卻不會自動回答「接下來由誰做什麼」。

以鯨豚為例，IMMA 用科學程序辨識重要海洋哺乳動物棲地；海龜專家小組則把區域族群的生物風險、威脅與保育能力放進優先評估。兩者都能整理複雜證據，讓有限資源有較清楚的討論起點。

但從地圖走到成果，至少還需要一條管理鏈：主管機關選擇適用的法律或合作工具，與受影響的漁業、航運、觀光或沿岸社群協商，定義可執行的措施，建立基線與指標，再定期公開成效並修正。任何一環缺席，都可能讓「優先區」停留在報告裡。

目前這份比較沒有證據顯示臺灣正式採用上述任一框架，因此只能作為制度思考的參照。看到一張保育優先地圖時，不妨沿著六個問題往下追：證據是什麼？誰有權決定？採取什麼措施？誰負責執行？如何監測？何時調整？

## English adaptation

A conservation map is good at answering where attention may be needed. It does not automatically answer who must do what next.

IMMAs identify habitat important to marine mammals. The marine-turtle portfolio compares regional units through biological risk, threats, and conservation capacity. Both frameworks can organise complex evidence and focus discussion.

Moving from a map to an outcome requires a management chain: authority, a legal or voluntary tool, consultation, an implementable measure, a baseline, indicators, public reporting, and adjustment. If those links are missing, a priority area may remain only a finding in a report.

This comparison does not establish that Taiwan has formally adopted either framework. Treat it as a way to ask better questions: What is the evidence? Who has authority? What measure follows? Who implements it? How is change monitored? When is the approach revised?
