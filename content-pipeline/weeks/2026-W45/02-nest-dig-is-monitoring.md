# 海龜巢穴開挖，是監測工作，不是體驗式救援

## Fact card

### Proposed angle

Explain post-hatching nest excavation as a controlled monitoring method and separate it from public wildlife handling.

### Central claim

A trained post-hatching nest evaluation can estimate hatch success and record nest conditions; it does not authorise the public to open nests or handle hatchlings.

### Confirmed facts

- Sea Turtle Conservancy describes public morning nest digs held after a nest has hatched.
- The programme evaluates nests and records information used to monitor sea-turtle populations and nesting-beach conditions.
- The public activity is organised around trained programme staff rather than independent handling by visitors.
- STC's site terms limit its materials to personal non-commercial use and restrict republication, derivative commercial use, and automated extraction.

### Uncertainty and limits

- This package does not describe the permit requirements, timing rules, or data protocol for Taiwan nest monitoring.
- Hatch success at one nest or beach does not by itself measure a population trend.
- Public access and wildlife-handling rules vary by jurisdiction and site.

### Taiwan connection

The connection is comparable. Taiwan readers can learn the difference between observing an authorised monitoring activity and intervening independently, but local guidance must come from the responsible authority and permitted teams.

### Reader action

If you encounter a sea-turtle nest or hatchlings, keep distance, avoid lights and handling, and follow local authority guidance. Do not interpret an organised nest evaluation as permission to dig.

### Claims not to publish

- Anyone can open a nest after hatchlings emerge.
- A nest dig guarantees a live-hatchling encounter or release.
- Hatch success at one event proves the population is recovering.
- STC photos, tracking data, or text may be reused without permission.

## 繁體中文草稿

海龜巢穴開挖容易被拍成一場近距離活動，但它的核心不是「讓大家接觸海龜」，而是由受訓人員在適當時間進行的孵化後監測。

Sea Turtle Conservancy 的活動說明指出，巢穴孵化後的評估可以記錄孵化成功情況與沙灘條件，資料用於長期監測。這些紀錄必須放回時間、地點與方法中解讀；單一巢穴的結果不能直接代表整個族群正在增加或減少。

公開觀察一場由專業團隊主持的活動，也不等於民眾可以自行挖掘巢穴、移動卵或接觸幼龜。過早開挖、燈光、踩踏與不當處理都可能造成風險，而且各地許可與程序不同。

對臺灣讀者最重要的比較不是複製活動，而是分清楚「觀察監測」與「自行介入」。遇到巢穴或幼龜時，應保持距離、減少燈光與干擾，依當地主管機關與獲准團隊的指示處理。

## English adaptation

A sea-turtle nest excavation can look like a close-up wildlife experience. Its core purpose, however, is a controlled post-hatching evaluation conducted by trained people at an appropriate time.

Sea Turtle Conservancy describes nest digs that record hatch success and beach conditions for monitoring. A result from one nest must be interpreted with its place, time, and method; it does not establish a population trend on its own.

Watching an organised evaluation does not authorise visitors to dig, move eggs, or handle hatchlings. Timing, lights, trampling, and improper handling can create risk, and permits differ across jurisdictions.

The comparable lesson for Taiwan is to separate observing authorised monitoring from intervening independently. Keep distance, reduce light and disturbance, and follow guidance from the responsible authority and permitted teams.
