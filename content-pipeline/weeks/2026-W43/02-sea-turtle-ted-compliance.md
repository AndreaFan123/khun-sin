# 海龜逃脫裝置不是裝上就算完成

## Fact card

### Proposed angle

Explain turtle excluder devices as an operating system of gear design, training, inspection, repair, and enforcement rather than a one-time equipment purchase.

### Central claim

A bycatch-reduction device works only when fishers install it correctly and institutions maintain compliance through training, inspection, repair, and enforcement.

### Confirmed facts

- NOAA Fisheries published the feature on 18 June 2026 and lists an update on 23 September 2026.
- A turtle excluder device creates an opening that lets sea turtles and other large animals leave a shrimp trawl while shrimp continue toward the end of the net.
- NOAA reports compliance above 90 percent in the Gulf shrimp fishery covered by its Gear Monitoring Team.
- The programme includes classroom training, on-water inspections, dockside outreach, and work with state and federal enforcement officers.
- NOAA says that officers and gear specialists repaired minor violations during one training patrol so vessels could continue fishing with compliant gear.

### Uncertainty and limits

- The compliance figure is a NOAA programme claim. This prototype did not independently audit the calculation or sampling method.
- The source concerns a United States shrimp fishery. Gear, target species, vessel design, enforcement authority, and operating conditions differ across fisheries.
- The source does not show that the same device or programme can be copied directly into Taiwan.

### Taiwan connection

The connection is comparable. Taiwan also faces questions about marine-animal bycatch, but a local recommendation requires fishery-specific evidence. The useful lesson is institutional: equipment standards need training, field checks, repair capacity, and feedback from fishers and enforcement staff.

### Reader action

When reading a claim about conservation gear, ask four questions: Which fishery uses it? How is correct installation checked? Who repairs failures? What evidence measures animal outcomes and fishing operations?

### Claims not to publish

- Turtle excluder devices solve all sea turtle bycatch.
- The reported compliance rate proves a matching reduction in turtle mortality.
- Taiwan should adopt the same device without local trials and consultation.
- Every image on the NOAA page is free to reuse.

## 繁體中文草稿

海龜逃脫裝置是在拖網中設置柵格與開口，讓海龜與其他大型動物有機會離開漁網。它常被介紹成一項有效的漁具改良，但裝置本身只是保育措施的一部分。

NOAA Fisheries 在 2026 年的一篇專題中，記錄美國墨西哥灣沿岸蝦拖網漁業的執行方式。該機構表示，所涵蓋漁業的裝置合規率已超過九成。這個成果不是只靠規定船上必須有設備。計畫還包含漁民教育、港邊檢查、海上登船、執法人員訓練與現場修正。

這個案例的重要之處，是把「有沒有裝」改成「能不能正確運作」。如果裝置角度、開口或網具配置不符合要求，海龜仍可能無法逃脫。檢查人員也要知道如何判定，漁民則需要能在出海前發現並修復問題。

這不代表臺灣可以直接複製同一套設備。不同漁法、船型、作業海域與混獲物種，需要各自的測試與管理設計。對臺灣的可比啟示是：保育漁具若缺少訓練、檢查、維修與成效資料，就可能只剩一項紙面要求。

下次看到「新漁具可以減少混獲」的消息，可以多問四件事：它用在哪一種漁業？誰檢查安裝是否正確？故障時誰能修？成效是用設備合規率、混獲率，還是動物死亡率衡量？

## English adaptation

A turtle excluder device adds bars and an opening to a trawl net so sea turtles and other large animals can escape while shrimp continue toward the cod end. The device is often presented as a technical fix. A 2026 NOAA Fisheries programme report shows why the surrounding operating system matters just as much.

NOAA reports compliance above 90 percent in the Gulf shrimp fishery covered by its Gear Monitoring Team. The programme combines fisher education, dockside checks, at-sea inspections, enforcement training, and on-site repair. During one training patrol, officers and gear specialists corrected minor problems so the vessels could continue fishing with compliant gear.

The case changes the question from whether a vessel carries a device to whether the device works as intended. Incorrect angles, openings, or net configurations can undermine the escape route. Inspectors need a consistent way to assess the gear, and crews need practical repair support.

Taiwan should not copy the equipment or programme without fishery-specific trials. Target catch, vessels, operating conditions, and bycatch risks differ. The comparable lesson is that a gear rule needs training, inspection, repair capacity, and outcome data.

When a conservation-gear claim appears, ask which fishery uses it, how installation is checked, who fixes failures, and whether the evidence measures compliance, bycatch, or animal survival.
